The Paddock Culture

More than a garage. A place where people build together.

The lifts and tools get people through the door. The culture is what makes them come back.

Whether you are changing your first set of brake pads or building a dedicated track car, Paddock is meant to feel clean, capable, welcoming, and shared. Everyone starts somewhere. Everyone belongs here.

Built by enthusiasts. Open to everyone.
01 — RESPECT

Every person. Every vehicle.

A Ferrari, an old Civic, a work truck, or a family SUV all deserve the same respect. Skill level and budget never determine who belongs.

02 — LEARN

Knowledge is meant to be shared.

Ask questions without embarrassment. Teach without ego. Celebrate progress, especially when someone is doing it for the first time.

03 — BUILD

Better cars. Better skills. Better community.

Paddock is a place to finish projects, gain confidence, meet people, and leave more capable than when you arrived.

04 — STEWARDSHIP

Leave the shop better than you found it.

Work safely, care for shared equipment, keep bays clean, and help protect the space for the person working beside you and the person coming next.

FAQ

What future members want to know.

Do I need professional mechanic experience?

No. Paddock is being designed for a wide range of skill levels. All users will complete a safety orientation before using the shop.

Will tools be included?

Standard rolling toolboxes are planned to be included with bay access. Specialty tools and equipment may carry an additional rental charge.

Can I bring my own tools and parts?

Yes. Members and guests will be able to bring their own tools, parts, fluids, and supplies, subject to shop safety and disposal policies.

Where will Paddock be located?

The final site has not been selected. Waitlist ZIP codes and travel preferences will help determine the best DMV location.

Can I leave a vehicle overnight?

Limited project storage may be offered for approved multi-day jobs. Availability, pricing, and policies will be announced before opening.

Is Paddock a repair shop?

No. Paddock provides the space, lifts, and tools for customers to work on their own vehicles. It is not a traditional mechanic or repair service.
